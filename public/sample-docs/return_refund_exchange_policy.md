Gadget Grove Return, Refund and Exchange Policy

# 1. Purpose and Scope
This section defines purpose and scope.

## 1.1 Purpose

This policy explains when and how customers can return products purchased from Gadget Grove, how refunds are calculated and paid, and how exchanges work. It is the single source of truth used by the Customer Support team. If another document or a customer-facing web page contradicts this policy, this policy applies unless a regional addendum in Section 11 says otherwise.

## 1.2 What This Policy Covers

- Products sold and shipped by Gadget Grove ("Gadget Grove-fulfilled" items)
- Products sold by Gadget Grove retail stores and returned online or in store
- Gift purchases and gift returns
- Defective, damaged, and incorrectly shipped items

## 1.3 What This Policy Does Not Cover

- Items sold by third-party marketplace sellers that are not fulfilled by Gadget Grove (see Section 3.5)

- Subscription and Gadget Grove Plus membership refunds (see the Subscription and Billing Handbook)

# 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Return window** | The number of days after the delivery date during which a return may be started. |
| **Delivery date** | The date the carrier scan shows "Delivered", or the pickup date for store pickup orders. |
| **RMA** | Return Merchandise Authorization. A unique number (format RMA-XXXXXXXX) generated when a return is approved. |
| **Original payment method** | The card, wallet, or account used to pay for the order. |
| **Store credit** | A balance stored in the customer's Gadget Grove account that can be used on future purchases. |
| **Restocking fee** | A percentage deducted from the refund for items that cannot be resold as new. |
| **Grade A / B / C** | The condition rating assigned during inspection (see Section 6.2). |
| **Final sale** | An item marked "Final Sale" on the product page and in the order confirmation. It cannot be returned. |

# 3. Return Eligibility
This section explains return eligibility.

## 3.1 Return Windows by Category

| Product Category | Return Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing, shoes, accessories | 30 days | Tags must be attached |
| Home and kitchen | 30 days | Unused and in original packaging |
| Furniture and large items | 30 days | Return pickup fee may apply (Section 5.2) |
| Smartphones, tablets, laptops | 14 days | Must be factory reset and signed out of all accounts |
| Headphones, speakers, wearables | 14 days | Ear tips and accessories must be included |
| Cameras and drones | 14 days | Battery and storage cards must be included |
| Books, toys, games | 30 days | Opened video games can only be exchanged for the same title |
| Beauty and personal care | 30 days | Unopened and sealed only |
| Grocery and perishables | Not returnable | Report problems within 48 hours for a refund |

During the holiday season (November 1 to December 31), the return window for most categories is extended until **January 31** of the following year. Electronics keep their standard 14-day window unless the order was delivered after December 17.

## 3.2 Condition Requirements

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- In its original condition, unused or lightly tried on
- In its original packaging, including inner boxes, protective film, manuals, and all accessories
- Free of odors, stains, pet hair, and damage not present at delivery
- Accompanied by the RMA number, either printed on the label or written on the package

Packaging that has been taped over or marked is acceptable. Missing original boxes for electronics reduce the refund (see Section 6.2).

## 3.3 Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned for change of mind:

- Gift cards, e-gift cards, and digital downloads
- Perishable goods such as food, flowers, and live plants
- Personalized, engraved, or made-to-order products
- Opened hygiene products, including earbuds with removable tips, razors, and intimate apparel
- Opened software, unless defective
- Hazardous materials such as aerosols, batteries sold loose, and flammable liquids
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

Items in this list can still be returned if they arrive defective, damaged, or are not what the customer ordered.

## 3.4 Final Sale and Clearance

Clearance items are returnable unless they display the "Final Sale" badge. The badge is shown on the product page, in the cart, and in the order confirmation email. Agents should check the order confirmation, not just the product page, because the badge can be added after the order is placed only in exceptional cases and never to orders already confirmed.

## 3.5 Marketplace Seller Items

Products sold by third-party sellers are labeled "Sold by [Seller name]". These items follow the seller's return policy, which is shown on the product page. Gadget Grove guarantees a minimum return window of 30 days for marketplace items under the **Gadget Grove Buyer Protection Program**. If a seller refuses a valid return, the customer can open a Buyer Protection case, and Gadget Grove will refund the customer and recover the cost from the seller.

# 4. Starting a Return

## 4.1 Online Return Request

1. Sign in to your account and open **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Return or Replace Items**.
3. Choose the items and the return reason from the list.
4. Choose a return method: drop-off, scheduled pickup, or in-store return.
5. Print the prepaid label or show the QR code at the drop-off point.
6. Pack the item securely and attach the label.

The RMA number is valid for **14 days**. If the label expires, the customer can request a new one without restarting the return, provided the return window has not closed.

## 4.2 Guest Checkout Returns

Customers who ordered without an account can start a return at gadgetgrove.example/returns using their order number and the email address used at checkout. Agents can also create the return on the customer's behalf.

## 4.3 Phone and Chat Returns

Support agents can create an RMA by verifying the customer's identity (order number plus email or billing ZIP code). Agents must never ask for a full card number or password. See the Account and Security Help Center for the verification checklist.

## 4.4 In-Store Returns

Items bought online can be returned to any Gadget Grove store. The customer must bring the order confirmation (digital or printed) and a photo ID for orders over $250. Refunds for in-store returns are processed immediately to the original payment method, usually appearing within 3 to 5 business days.

# 5. Return Shipping

## 5.1 Who Pays for Return Shipping

| Return Reason | Return Shipping Cost |
|---|---|
| Item defective or damaged | Free (Gadget Grove pays) |
| Wrong item sent | Free (Gadget Grove pays) |
| Item not as described | Free (Gadget Grove pays) |
| Changed my mind, Gadget Grove Plus member | Free |
| Changed my mind, standard customer | $5.99 deducted from the refund |
| Size or color exchange | Free |

## 5.2 Large and Heavy Items

Items over 50 lb (23 kg) or longer than 48 inches (122 cm) are returned by scheduled freight pickup. The first pickup is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, a pickup fee of $49 is deducted from the refund. Customers must keep the original packaging where possible; if it is unavailable, a repacking fee of $25 may apply.

## 5.3 International Returns

Customers outside the United States return items to the regional returns center listed on their label. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was defective. Customs forms are included in the label package. Refunds are issued only after the item clears customs and is inspected.

## 5.4 Lost Return Packages

Customers should keep the drop-off receipt and tracking number. If a return package is lost in transit and the tracking shows it was accepted by the carrier, Gadget Grove will issue the refund after the carrier confirms the loss, typically within 10 business days. Agents should open a carrier trace and tell the customer the expected timeline.

# 6. Inspection and Processing

## 6.1 Processing Timeline

Returned items are received at a returns center, scanned, and inspected. The standard timeline is:

| Stage | Typical Time |
|---|---|
| In transit to returns center | 3 to 7 business days |
| Received and scanned | Same day as arrival |
| Inspection | 1 to 2 business days |
| Refund issued | Within 1 business day after inspection |
| Refund appears with the bank | 3 to 10 business days, depending on the method |

Customers receive an email at each stage. Refunds for Gadget Grove Plus members are issued as soon as the carrier scan shows the item in transit.

## 6.2 Condition Grading

| Grade | Description | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Like new, complete with original packaging | 100% of item price |
| **B** | Minor wear, missing non-essential packaging, or opened box | 85% to 95% of item price |
| **C** | Visible wear, missing accessories, or damage caused by the customer | 50% to 75% of item price |
| **Rejected** | Used beyond reasonable inspection, missing major parts, or not the item sold | No refund; item returned to the customer on request |

Deductions are always explained in the refund email. The customer has 14 days to dispute the grade by replying with photos of the item as shipped.

# 7. Refunds

## 7.1 Refund Method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. The exceptions are:

- **Gift card purchases:** refunded to a new gift card
- **Expired or closed cards:** refunded by bank transfer or store credit, at the customer's choice
- **Cash on delivery orders:** refunded by bank transfer or store credit
- **Orders paid partly with store credit:** the store credit portion is refunded to store credit

## 7.2 Refund Timelines by Payment Method

| Payment Method | After Refund Is Issued |
|---|---|
| Visa, Mastercard, American Express | 5 to 7 business days |
| Debit card | 5 to 10 business days |
| PayPal | 3 to 5 business days |
| Apple Pay, Google Pay | 5 to 7 business days |
| Bank transfer | 3 to 5 business days |
| Store credit | Immediate |
| Gift card | Immediate |

## 7.3 What Is Refunded

The refund includes the item price and the tax paid on that item. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to Gadget Grove error (defective, damaged, or wrong item), or when an entire order is returned within 48 hours of delivery. Expedited shipping fees are refunded only for Gadget Grove errors.

## 7.4 Partial Order Returns and Promotions

If a promotion required a minimum order value, returning an item can cause the order to fall below the threshold. In this case:

- The discount is recalculated proportionally for the remaining items.
- If the discount was a free gift with purchase, the gift must be returned or its value deducted.
- Percentage discounts are refunded at the discounted price, not the original price.

## 7.5 Currency Differences

Refunds are issued in the currency of the original payment. Exchange-rate differences between the purchase date and refund date are the responsibility of the customer's bank and are not adjusted by Gadget Grove.

# 8. Exchanges

## 8.1 How Exchanges Work

Customers can exchange an item for a different size, color, or the same product within 30 days. An exchange ships as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier, as long as the customer has a valid payment method on file. If the replacement is more expensive, the customer is charged the difference. If it is cheaper, the difference is refunded.

## 8.2 Out-of-Stock Replacements

If the requested replacement is out of stock, the customer can choose between a full refund, store credit with a 10% bonus, or waiting for the item to be back in stock. Backorders are held for up to 21 days.

## 8.3 Exchanging Electronics

Electronics can be exchanged only for the same model if defective. Switching to a different model is treated as a return and a new purchase.

# 9. Special Situations

## 9.1 Defective Items

If a product stops working within the return window, the customer chooses between a refund and a replacement. After the return window, defective items are handled under the Warranty and Repair Service Manual. Agents should ask for a short description and, where relevant, a photo or video.

## 9.2 Wrong Item Received

Ship the correct item immediately at no cost and send a prepaid return label for the wrong one. Do not require the wrong item to arrive before the replacement ships. Customers may keep low-value items (under $15) at their request, and the agent should record this as reason code WRG-KEEP.

## 9.3 Damaged in Transit

Ask for photos of the packaging and the damage. Issue a replacement or refund without requiring the damaged item to be returned if the item is under $75. For higher-value items, issue a return label.

## 9.4 Missing Parts or Accessories

Send the missing part when possible. If the part is not available, offer a partial refund proportional to the value of the missing part, or a full return.

## 9.5 Late Returns

Requests up to 7 days after the window may be approved as store credit by an agent. Requests beyond 7 days require a team lead. Gadget Grove Plus members receive an additional 7 days on all return windows.

## 9.6 Gift Returns

Recipients with a gift receipt (or the order number) can return the item for store credit at the price paid. The original purchaser is not notified. If the recipient has no gift receipt, the item can be exchanged at the current selling price or refunded as store credit at the lowest price in the last 90 days.

## 9.7 Price Adjustments

If an item's price drops within 14 days of delivery, the customer can request a price adjustment for the difference. Adjustments are not available for clearance items, limited-time deals, or marketplace sellers.

# 10. Fraud Prevention and Return Limits

## 10.1 Return Limits

To protect Gadget Grove from abuse, accounts that return more than **40% of their orders by value** over a rolling 12-month period (with at least 5 orders) may be reviewed. A review may result in store credit only refunds, restricted return methods, or account closure. Agents should not mention this threshold to customers.

## 10.2 Suspicious Return Patterns

The following are flagged for review:

- Returning items different from those shipped
- Repeated "not received" claims followed by purchase of the same item
- Returns with substantially altered or used goods
- Multiple accounts using the same address or payment method

## 10.3 Agent Responsibilities

Agents must follow the documented process and never accuse a customer of fraud. If a case looks suspicious, add the tag FRAUD-REVIEW and hand it to the Trust and Safety team.

# 11. Regional Variations

## 11.1 United States

The policy in this document applies in full. Some states, including California and New York, require clear display of return policies at checkout. Sales tax is refunded in all cases.

## 11.2 Canada

Return windows match the US. Returns are accepted at Gadget Grove stores in Canada. Refunds include GST/HST/PST as paid.

## 11.3 European Union and United Kingdom

Customers have a statutory **14-day right of withdrawal** from the date of delivery, regardless of the return window above. If the customer asks for a refund under this right, Gadget Grove will refund the full price including standard delivery charges. Customers pay return shipping unless the item was defective. Custom-made items and sealed hygiene products are excluded under consumer law.

## 11.4 Australia and New Zealand

Statutory consumer guarantees apply to faulty items. Customers are entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund for major faults regardless of the return window.

# 12. Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I return an item without the original box?

For clothing and home items, yes. For electronics and small appliances, the box is required to receive full value. Without the box, expect a Grade B or C deduction.

## Can I return part of an order?

Yes. Select only the items you want to return. Promotions on the remaining items may be recalculated (Section 7.4).

## My return label expired. What should I do?

Open the return in **My Orders** and click **Get New Label**. If your return window has closed, contact support.

## How do I know my return was received?

You will get an email when the package is scanned at the returns center. You can also check the status under **My Orders > Returns**.

## Can I return an item bought with a gift card?

Yes. The refund goes back to a new gift card with the same balance.

## The refund amount is lower than I expected.

Check the refund email for the grading breakdown and any shipping or restocking deduction. You can dispute the grade within 14 days by replying to the email with photos.

## Do you refund the shipping cost?

Only when the return was caused by our error, or the full order was returned within 48 hours of delivery.
