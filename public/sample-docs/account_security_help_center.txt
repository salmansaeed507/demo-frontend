1. CREATING AND MANAGING YOUR ACCOUNT
=====================================================================

1.1 Who can create an account
-----------------------------
Anyone aged 16 or over can create a Gadget Grove account. Customers aged
13 to 15 may use an account only with a parent or guardian listed as
the account holder. Accounts for children under 13 are not allowed.

1.2 How to create an account
----------------------------
  1. Go to gadgetgrove.example or open the Gadget Grove mobile app.
  2. Tap "Sign up".
  3. Enter your email address or phone number.
  4. Choose a password with at least 12 characters.
  5. Enter the 6-digit verification code we send you.
  6. Add your name and, optionally, a shipping address.

You can also sign up using Apple, Google or Facebook. Social sign-in
links your Gadget Grove account to that provider. You can later add a
Gadget Grove password in Settings > Security.

1.3 Guest checkout
------------------
You can buy without an account. Guest orders can be tracked using the
order number and email address. Guest orders do not collect Gadget Grove
Plus benefits, store credit, or saved addresses. You can convert a
guest order into an account by signing up with the same email address.
Orders from the last 90 days are linked automatically.

1.4 Multiple accounts
---------------------
Each person may hold one account. If you created a second account by
mistake, contact support to merge them. Merging combines order history
and store credit. Gadget Grove Plus memberships cannot be merged; the
membership with the later renewal date is kept and the other is
cancelled with a prorated refund.

2. SIGNING IN
=====================================================================

2.1 Where you can sign in
-------------------------
You can sign in on the website, the Android and iOS apps, and the
in-store kiosks. A single session is kept active for 30 days on
trusted devices and 24 hours on shared or public devices.

2.2 Sign-in methods
-------------------
  - Email and password
  - Phone number and one-time code
  - Passkey (fingerprint, face or device PIN)
  - Apple, Google or Facebook

2.3 Common sign-in problems
---------------------------

Q: It says "Incorrect email or password" but I am sure they are right.
A: Check whether your keyboard layout, caps lock, or autofill is
   adding extra characters. Try signing in with a phone number or
   passkey if you have set them up. If you signed up with Apple or
   Google, use that button instead of typing a password.

Q: I get "Account not found".
A: You may have used a different email address. Try each of your
   email addresses. Search your inbox for "Welcome to Gadget Grove" to
   find the address you used.

Q: The page keeps sending me back to the sign-in screen.
A: Browser cookies may be blocked. Allow cookies for gadgetgrove.example,
   clear your browser cache, or try a private window. Make sure your
   device date and time are correct.

Q: My account is locked.
A: After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30
   minutes. After 10 failed attempts in 24 hours it is locked until
   you reset your password. See Section 3.

2.4 Passkeys
------------
A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face, or device
PIN, with no password. To add one, go to Settings > Security >
Passkeys > Add a passkey. Passkeys are stored on your device or in
your password manager and are never sent to Gadget Grove. You can
register up to 10 passkeys.

3. PASSWORD PROBLEMS
=====================================================================

3.1 Password requirements
-------------------------
  - At least 12 characters
  - Not one of the 10,000 most common passwords
  - Not the same as your email address or name
  - Not a password you used in the past 5 changes

We recommend using a password manager. We do not require special
characters or periodic password changes.

3.2 Resetting a forgotten password
----------------------------------
  1. On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password".
  2. Enter your email address or phone number.
  3. Open the reset message. The link and code are valid for 30
     minutes and can be used only once.
  4. Choose a new password and confirm it.
  5. You are signed out of all other devices automatically.

3.3 I did not receive the reset email
-------------------------------------
  - Check your spam, junk and promotions folders.
  - Search for "noreply@gadgetgrove.example".
  - Make sure you typed the same address you registered with.
  - Wait 5 minutes before requesting another message. Only the most
    recent link works.
  - Add noreply@gadgetgrove.example to your contacts.
  - If you use a work email, your company filter may be blocking the
    message. Ask your IT team to allow our domain.

3.4 Changing your password
--------------------------
Go to Settings > Security > Password. You will be asked to enter
your current password. If you signed up with social sign-in and have
never set a password, choose "Create password" instead.

3.5 Password reset by support
-----------------------------
Support agents cannot see, send, or set your password. An agent can
send a reset link to the email address or phone number on file after
verification (see Section 6). If you no longer have access to either,
see Section 5.6, "Account recovery without access to email".

4. TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (2FA)
=====================================================================

4.1 What 2FA does
-----------------
Two-factor authentication asks for a second proof of identity (a
code or approval) in addition to your password. It protects your
account if your password is stolen.

4.2 Available 2FA methods
-------------------------
  Method                    Notes
  ------------------------  ------------------------------------------
  Authenticator app         Recommended. Works offline. Compatible with
                            any TOTP app.
  Passkey / security key    Strongest option. Phishing-resistant.
  SMS code                  Convenient but vulnerable to SIM swapping.
  Backup codes              10 single-use codes for emergencies.

4.3 Turning on 2FA
------------------
  1. Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication.
  2. Select a method.
  3. For an authenticator app, scan the QR code and enter the 6-digit
     code shown in the app.
  4. Download and save your backup codes.
  5. Confirm by entering a code once.

4.4 I lost my phone
-------------------
  - Use one of your backup codes to sign in.
  - Then go to Settings > Security and remove the lost device.
  - Set up 2FA again on your new phone.
  - If you have no backup codes, you must complete account recovery
    (see Section 5.6). This takes 3 to 5 business days for security
    reasons.

4.5 Codes not working
---------------------
  - Authenticator codes depend on your phone's clock. Turn on
    automatic date and time.
  - SMS codes expire after 10 minutes.
  - Entering a wrong code 5 times locks 2FA for 15 minutes.
  - Never share a code with anyone. Gadget Grove will never ask for it.

4.6 Removing 2FA
----------------
You can turn off 2FA in Settings > Security. For accounts with
Gadget Grove Plus or a stored payment method, you must re-enter your
password first. A notification is sent to your email when 2FA is
removed.

5. ACCOUNT SECURITY AND SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY
=====================================================================

5.1 Signs that your account may be compromised
----------------------------------------------
  - Orders you did not place
  - Changed email address, phone number, or shipping address
  - Password reset emails you did not request
  - Sign-in alerts from unfamiliar locations or devices
  - Gift cards or store credit that has disappeared

5.2 What to do right away
-------------------------
  1. Reset your password from the sign-in page.
  2. Go to Settings > Security > Devices and choose "Sign out of
     all devices".
  3. Turn on two-factor authentication.
  4. Review recent orders and saved payment methods.
  5. Contact support using the form at gadgetgrove.example/security so
     we can freeze the account and review activity.
  6. Contact your bank if a payment card was used.

5.3 What Gadget Grove will do
-------------------------
When you report a compromise, we will:
  - Temporarily lock the account to stop further changes.
  - Cancel unshipped orders that you did not place.
  - Refund unauthorized orders paid with your account.
  - Restore store credit that was spent without authorization.
  - Remove unknown devices and saved payment methods.

Reviews usually finish within 2 business days. You will receive an
email when your account is safe to use again.

5.4 Phishing and scams
----------------------
Gadget Grove will never:
  - Ask for your password, full card number, or 2FA code by phone,
    email, text, or chat.
  - Ask you to pay using gift cards, cryptocurrency, or wire transfer.
  - Ask you to install software to "fix" an account problem.
  - Send you to a site that does not end in gadgetgrove.example.

If you get a suspicious message, forward it to
phishing@gadgetgrove.example and delete it. If you clicked a link or
entered your details, reset your password immediately.

5.5 Sign-in alerts
------------------
We email you when a sign-in occurs from a new device or country.
If it was not you, use the "This wasn't me" button in the email to
lock the account and start a reset.

5.6 Account recovery without access to email
--------------------------------------------
If you can no longer access the email address or phone number on
your account:
  1. Go to gadgetgrove.example/recover.
  2. Provide your name, the last 4 digits of a payment card used,
     and the shipping address from a recent order.
  3. Upload a photo ID (it is deleted within 30 days of the review).
  4. Wait 3 to 5 business days for our security team to respond.
You will receive a message at the new email address you provide.

5.7 Compromised payment cards
-----------------------------
Gadget Grove does not store full card numbers. We keep a token and the
last 4 digits. If you believe a card was misused, contact your bank
to block it, then remove the card from Settings > Payments.


6. IDENTITY VERIFICATION FOR SUPPORT REQUESTS

6.1 Why we verify
-----------------
We verify identity before sharing or changing account information to
protect your privacy.

6.2 Verification levels
-----------------------

  Level 1 (general information, order status)
      Order number AND email address or billing ZIP code.

  Level 2 (address change, cancel order, refund to new method)
      Level 1 plus one-time code sent to the email or phone number
      on file.

  Level 3 (change email or phone, remove 2FA, account recovery)
      Level 2 plus security questions or ID verification.

6.3 What agents never ask for
-----------------------------
  - Passwords or one-time codes (except via the verified channel
    process above, where the customer reads the code displayed on
    their own device and the agent only checks that it matches)
  - Full card numbers, CVV, or PINs
  - Social security or national ID numbers
  - Remote access to the customer's device

7. UPDATING PROFILE, ADDRESS AND PAYMENT DETAILS
=====================================================================

7.1 Changing your email address
-------------------------------
Go to Settings > Profile > Email. A confirmation link is sent to the
new address and a notice is sent to the old address. The change takes
effect after you confirm. For security, you cannot change your email
address more than twice in 30 days.

7.2 Changing your phone number
------------------------------
Go to Settings > Profile > Phone. Enter the new number and the code
that we send. If the number was used for 2FA, you must set up 2FA
again.

7.3 Address book
----------------
You can store up to 20 addresses. To edit or delete an address, go to
Settings > Addresses. Changes do not affect orders already in
progress. To change the address of an unshipped order, see the
Shipping and Delivery Guide, Section 7.

7.4 Payment methods
-------------------
You can save up to 10 payment methods. To add or remove a card, go to
Settings > Payments. Removing a card does not cancel subscriptions
that use it. Update the subscription's payment method first.

7.5 Changing your name
----------------------
Your name can be edited in Settings > Profile. The name on shipping
labels comes from the address book, not the profile.

8. PRIVACY, DATA AND ACCOUNT DELETION
=====================================================================

8.1 What data we hold
---------------------
We hold order history, saved addresses, contact details, a payment
token, support conversations, device information, and browsing
preferences. See our Privacy Notice at gadgetgrove.example/privacy.

8.2 Downloading your data
-------------------------
Go to Settings > Privacy > Download my data. A ZIP file will be
emailed to you within 48 hours. The download link expires after 7
days.

8.3 Deleting your account
-------------------------
  1. Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete account.
  2. Review what will be removed.
  3. Confirm with your password and a one-time code.

What happens next:
  - Your account is deactivated immediately.
  - You have 14 days to change your mind by signing in.
  - After 14 days, your profile, addresses, wishlists and store
    credit are permanently deleted.
  - Order records are kept for 7 years for tax and legal reasons but
    are disconnected from your personal profile where possible.

Before deleting: use any remaining store credit, cancel Gadget Grove
Plus, and complete open returns. Active subscriptions must be
cancelled first.

8.4 Regional privacy rights
---------------------------
Customers in the EU, UK and California have additional rights,
including access, correction, deletion, and objection to processing.
Requests can be made at privacy@gadgetgrove.example and are answered
within 30 days.

9. NOTIFICATIONS AND MARKETING PREFERENCES
=====================================================================

You control notifications under Settings > Notifications.

  Type                       Can be turned off?
  -------------------------  ----------------------------------------
  Order and shipping         Email: no. Push and SMS: yes.
  Security alerts            No.
  Marketing and promotions   Yes.
  Price drop alerts          Yes.
  Back-in-stock alerts       Yes.

To stop all marketing emails, click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of
any email. To stop SMS, reply STOP. It can take up to 48 hours for
the change to apply.

12. AGENT PROCEDURES
=====================================================================

12.1 Standard handling steps
----------------------------
  1. Greet the customer and ask for the order number and email.
  2. Verify identity at the correct level (Section 6).
  3. Identify whether the issue is sign-in, security, or profile.
  4. Follow the matching section of this document.
  5. Confirm the issue is resolved and summarize actions taken.
  6. Tag the case with the correct code (ACC-SIGNIN, ACC-SEC,
     ACC-PROFILE, ACC-DELETE).

12.2 When to escalate
---------------------
  - Suspected account takeover with financial loss: Security Team
  - Customer cannot access email or phone and has no backup codes:
    Account Recovery Team
  - Legal or privacy requests: Privacy Office
  - Abusive customers: follow the Conduct Policy

12.3 Do and do not
------------------
  DO     verify identity before disclosing information
  DO     use the standard reset link flow
  DO     document every security action in the case notes
  DO NOT read out a customer's full address or full email aloud
  DO NOT promise a time for security reviews under 2 business days
  DO NOT disable 2FA without Level 3 verification
